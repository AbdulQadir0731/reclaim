Common questions

The scan says "Nothing to clean" but my library is full

If "Optimize iPhone Storage" is on, many photos live in iCloud and only a small preview is on the device. Reclaim analyses what is local and tells you how many items it had to skip. Tap that notice to download and check them, or connect to Wi-Fi and try again.

Why does it want access to my whole photo library?

It cannot compare photos it is not allowed to see. If you grant access to only selected photos, Reclaim still works — it just only examines the photos you selected. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Reclaim.

I deleted something by mistake

Open the Photos app, go to Albums › Recently Deleted. iOS keeps deleted photos and videos there for about 30 days. Deleted mail is in your mailbox's Trash folder.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings › tap your name › Subscriptions › Reclaim › Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com — we have no ability to issue them ourselves.

I paid but the app still shows the free tier

Open Settings inside Reclaim and tap Restore purchases, making sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

I forgot my Secret Space PIN

There is no way to recover it, for us or for anyone. The encryption key never leaves your device and we hold no copy of your data. The only option is Settings › Delete Secret Space, which erases the contents and lets you start again.

My mailbox won't connect

Most providers require an app-specific password rather than your normal login password once two-factor authentication is on, and some require IMAP to be enabled in their web settings first. The app shows the right link for your provider on the connection screen. Proton Mail and Tutanota do not support standard IMAP at all, so they cannot be connected.

How do I disconnect a mailbox?

Sign out inside the email cleaner — that deletes the stored token or password from your device Keychain immediately. For a Google account you can additionally revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security or privacy flaw, please email us directly rather than posting publicly, and allow reasonable time to fix it before disclosure. We appreciate it.
