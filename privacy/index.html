Privacy Policy

Applies to the Reclaim iOS app (bundle identifier com.aq.reclaimphonecleaner) and this website.
Version 1.0 · Effective 10 September 2026

The short version. Reclaim has no server, no user account and no analytics. Your photos, videos and contacts are read and analysed entirely on your iPhone and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data, because we never receive any. The only network connections the app makes are to Apple for purchases and, if you choose to connect a mailbox, directly to your own mail provider.

1. Who we are

Reclaim is developed and published by Abdul Qadir, an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at dellqadir0731@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Reclaim operates no backend service. There is no sign-up, no profile, no advertising identifier, no third-party analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK in the app. We cannot see how you use the app, what is in your library, or that you installed it.

The table below lists every category of data the app touches on your device, why, and where it goes. "Stays on device" means the data never leaves your iPhone in any form.

DataWhy the app reads itWhere it goes
Photos & videos To find exact duplicates, near-identical shots, screenshots and oversized videos, and to show you thumbnails so you can choose what to delete. Stays on device. Analysis happens locally through Apple's Photos framework.
Image fingerprints Each photo is reduced to a small perceptual hash (about 60 bytes — not an image, and not reversible into one) so repeat scans are fast. Stays on device, in the app's private storage. Cleared by "Rescan from scratch" or by deleting the app.

3. Connecting a mailbox

The email cleaner is optional and does nothing until you connect an account yourself.

3.1 Google accounts

If you sign in with Google, Reclaim uses Google's standard OAuth 2.0 flow inside a secure system browser window. Reclaim never sees or stores your Google password. Google returns an access token, which is stored in your device Keychain and used only from your device.

Reclaim requests the minimum access it needs to do its job:

  • gmail.modify — to list message headers and to move the messages you select into the Trash label. Reclaim never permanently deletes mail, never reads message bodies or attachments, and never sends mail.
  • userinfo.email — to display which account you are signed in to.

Limited Use disclosure. Reclaim's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: Google user data is used only to provide the mail-cleaning features you can see in the app; it is never transferred to us or to any third party; it is never used for advertising, profiling or credit purposes; and no human ever reads it, except with your explicit consent for support, or as required by law.

You can revoke Reclaim's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by signing out inside the app, which deletes the stored token from your device.

3.2 Other mail providers (IMAP)

For non-Google mailboxes, Reclaim connects straight to your provider's IMAP server over an encrypted TLS connection, using the address and app password you enter. There is no intermediary — no traffic passes through any server we control, because we do not operate one. Your password is held in the iOS Keychain on that device only and is deleted when you remove the account in the app.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, card details or billing address. Apple tells the app only whether an active entitlement exists. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Who we share data with

Nobody. We have no data to share. Reclaim contains no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no attribution SDKs, no data brokers and no third-party code of any kind.

The only external parties involved at all are ones you choose to invoke, and they receive data directly from your device, not from us:

  • Apple — for App Store purchases.
  • Google — only if you connect a Google account, under the terms in section 3.1.
  • Your own mail provider — only if you add an IMAP mailbox.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act.

6. Retention and deletion

Because everything is stored locally, you are always in direct control of it:

  • Fingerprint cache — Settings › Rescan from scratch.
  • Cleanup history — Settings › Reset cleanup history.
  • Secret Space — Settings › Delete Secret Space erases every encrypted item and the PIN. This cannot be undone.
  • Mail account and credentials — sign out or remove the account in the email cleaner; the token or password is deleted from the Keychain immediately.
  • Everything at once — delete the Reclaim app. iOS removes the app's container, including all of the above.

We hold no copy of any of it, so there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf. If you would like written confirmation of that, email us and we will send it.

7. Your rights

Privacy laws including the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. We support those rights by design: we are not a controller of any personal data about you, because none is transmitted to us. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will investigate and respond within 30 days. EU and UK users also have the right to complain to their local data protection authority.

8. Children

Reclaim is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children, contains no social features, no user-generated content and no advertising, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone, children included.

9. Security

Credentials and encryption keys are held in the iOS Keychain with device-only accessibility, so they do not travel to another device in a backup. Secret Space items are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Mail connections use TLS. No security measure is perfect, but the strongest one here is structural: there is no server of ours to breach and no database of yours to steal.

If this policy changes materially, we will update the version and date at the top of this page and describe the change in the app's release notes. Continuing to use Reclaim after a change means you accept the updated policy.
