Free up iPhone storage, honestly.

Reclaim finds the duplicate photos, near-identical bursts, screenshots and oversized videos actually taking up your space — then shows you what you'd get back before anything is deleted.

What it actually does

No fake speed-ups, no invented "junk files", no RAM boosting. iOS does not let any app do those things, and apps that claim otherwise are selling you a progress bar. Reclaim only deletes real files you can see, and only after you confirm.

Your photos never leave your phone

Every scan runs locally on your device. Reclaim has no server, no account and no analytics. Photos are analysed into a 60-byte fingerprint that stays in the app's own storage — the images themselves are never uploaded, copied or transmitted anywhere.

The only time Reclaim touches the network is when you connect a mailbox to clean it, and then it talks directly to your own mail provider. Read the full detail in the privacy policy.

Nothing is deleted without you

Every deletion goes through one review screen. You see each item, the total space it will free, and which copy is being kept. Photos and videos are removed through the system Photos library, so they land in Recently Deleted and are recoverable for 30 days. Mail is moved to your mailbox's Trash, never permanently erased.

Free and Pro. Finding things is always free, and so is cleaning up to a daily limit. Reclaim Pro removes that daily limit and unlocks the extra tools. Prices, renewal terms and cancellation are shown in the app before you buy anything — see the Terms of Use.

Questions

Will this make my iPhone faster?

No, and no app can. Reclaim frees storage space. That's a different thing from speed, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise.

Can I get a deleted photo back?

Yes. Deletions go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days. Items you move into Secret Space are encrypted on your device instead; if you also chose to remove the original, the original goes to Recently Deleted as well.

Why does it ask for photo access?

It cannot compare your photos without reading them. You can grant access to your whole library or only selected photos — Reclaim works either way, it just has less to look at.

What is Secret Space?

An optional PIN-protected area inside Reclaim. Items you put there are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in your device's Keychain. There is no recovery if you forget the PIN — not by us, because we have no copy of anything.
